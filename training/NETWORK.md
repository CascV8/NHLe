Generated by `training/network.py` — don't edit by hand.

Seasons 2010-26 (regular season), 17,001 player-seasons in two or more leagues, 160 league pairs with 10+ shared players (10+ GP in each league).
Factor = NHL points per league point: the share-weighted sum of all the league's paths, each connector PPG / league PPG × the connector's NHLe (the NHL itself is the direct path, at 1). The connectors' values come from a first pass (`1st`) that solves leagues one at a time outward from the NHL, next the one sharing the most players with those already solved (`#` is the order); the factor then uses every connection a league has. `WLS` is a log-space least-squares solve over the same pairs, as a cross-check. `±` is the bootstrap standard error of the log factor (0.05 ≈ ±5%). `Players` counts shared player-seasons across the league's pairs, `NHL direct` those shared with the NHL, `from sites` those that came from league-website tables, `from EP` from Elite Prospects tables and `from HockeyDB` from HockeyDB rosters, rather than NHL career histories.
