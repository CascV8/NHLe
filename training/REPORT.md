## World Juniors terms

Log-odds added for a player who played the tournament: `played + per_pts84 × (his NHLe pts/84 there)`, where NHLe pts/84 = WJC points × the WJC factor / games × 84. Ridge 1.0 on these terms (a N(0, 1) prior on `played`, and on `per_pts84` per 20 pts/84), so a term with a handful of players stays near zero.
