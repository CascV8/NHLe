Generated by `training/seasons.py` — don't edit by hand.

The network factors ([NETWORK.md](NETWORK.md)), allowed to move by season: each league-season's crossovers, pooled per league pair, shift it from the league's network level, with a N(0, τ²) prior on the shift. τ is fit by maximum likelihood: **τ = 0.05** (σ = 1.08 per player). A τ near 0 means seasons don't differ by more than their crossovers' noise.

Rule change: from 2025-26 CHL players keep NCAA eligibility. The NCAA and the CHL leagues get a step from that season on, with a N(0, 0.15²) prior on its log, so it stays near 1 until seasons after the change say otherwise.
